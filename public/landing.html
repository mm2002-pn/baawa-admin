How it works

Four pillars. No one searches alone.

  1. 01

    Prevent

    A small device clipped to the school bag lets parents know their child is safe. In danger, the child can raise the alert.

  2. 02

    Report

    If a child goes missing, the family reports it for free in just minutes.

  3. 03

    Alert

    The alert is sent to the police and the community.

  4. 04

    Search

    Smart tools help the search, always under the authorities' control.
